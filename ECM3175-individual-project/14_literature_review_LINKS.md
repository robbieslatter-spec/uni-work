**How to use these links:**
- **DOI links** (https://doi.org/…) take you to the publisher's page. If it asks you to pay, open it through the University of Exeter library (search the title in the library search and log in) and you'll usually get the full text free.
- **Free copies** are open-access versions you can read straight away.
